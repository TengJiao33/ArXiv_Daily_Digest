## What We Actually Care About

The radar is most useful when papers connect to at least one of these concrete
research seams:

- Truthfulness and factuality: hallucination, factual recall, knowledge conflict,
  RAG faithfulness, claim verification, confidence calibration, abstention.
- Representation engineering: residual stream signals, activation steering,
  steering vectors, probes, function vectors, SAE features, intervention.
- Mechanistic interpretability: attention heads, circuits, causal patching,
  logit lens, sparse autoencoders, decodability versus causal use.

Papers are worth keeping even if they are outside the exact configured direction
when they offer a method or dataset that can transfer directly into those seams.
This is not a weak form of relevance: `method-transfer` papers stay in the
active corpus.

Important distinction:

- Remove/quarantine papers that are off-domain and do not contribute reusable
  methods, datasets, evaluations, or limitations.
- Keep papers that are off-main-topic but offer reusable machinery, such as
  probes, activation interventions, causal patching, SAE analysis, claim
  verification, calibration metrics, retrieval policies, or agent-memory
  evaluation.

## Recommended Active Corpus Definition

For near-term research-map analysis, use:

- `core`: directly about truthfulness, hallucination, faithfulness, internal
  representation, steering, mechanistic interpretability, RAG verification, or
  agent memory safety.
- `method-transfer`: not about truthfulness, but the method can be reused for
  probes, steering, activation patching, SAE analysis, claim verification,
  calibration, retrieval policy, or agent-memory evaluation. This is an active
  keep status.

Exclude from active analysis:

- Domain ML papers whose target is wireless, finance, remote sensing, crystal
  growth, protein design, speech/TTS, or broad governance and that do not offer a
  reusable technical method for our active seams.
